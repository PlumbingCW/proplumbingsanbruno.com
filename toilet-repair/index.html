Emergency Toilet Repair in San Bruno, CA

An overflowing or leaking toilet cannot wait. We answer 24/7, repair running and leaking toilets and install new ones.

  • 24/7 emergency line
  • Repair and replace
  • Same-day visits

A toilet that overflows, leaks at the floor or will not stop running needs fast attention. Stop the water at the shutoff valve behind the toilet, and keep the lid and bowl clear. We answer 24/7 and send a plumber the same day, including nights and weekends.

Tell us what the toilet is doing. A running tank is a quick part swap. A leak at the base is a reset. A repeated clog may mean a drain problem, and that changes what we bring.

Toilet overflowing: what to do first

  1. Lift the tank lid and press the flapper closed to stop the flow.
  2. Turn the shutoff valve behind the toilet clockwise.
  3. Do not flush again. Mop standing water and ventilate the room.
  4. If other fixtures are gurgling, the problem is in the drain line. Stop using water and call us.

Toilet leak and running toilet repair

A running toilet can waste a surprising amount of water without any noise. A quick test is to drop a few drops of food coloring in the tank and wait. If color shows in the bowl without flushing, the flapper is leaking.

San Bruno's water bill goes through the city's utility billing office. If your bill has climbed and no one is home, check each toilet first. It is the most common hidden leak in a house, and fixing it is inexpensive.

Toilet installation and replacement

Replacing a toilet is a good choice for an old or cracked unit. We remove the old toilet, check the flange and subfloor, set the new toilet on a fresh wax ring, level it and connect the supply line. A flange that is broken or set too low needs repair first.

Newer toilets use less water per flush and many have taller bowls for comfort. We help you choose a model that fits your bathroom's rough-in distance, which is the measurement from the wall to the center of the drain.

When the problem is the drain, not the toilet

If several toilets clog or the tub gurgles when you flush, the drain line is partially blocked. A plunger gives only temporary relief. A cable or camera finds the cause. For that work, see drain cleaning.

If sewage is backing up through the floor drain or the lowest toilet, treat it as a sewer line emergency. See sewer line repair.

Older bathrooms near El Camino Real

Houses built before the 1990s often have toilets that use far more water per flush than newer ones, and older shutoff valves that seize when turned. We replace stiff or leaking shutoff valves during a repair so the next problem is easier to handle.

Condos and apartments often share drain stacks. If a clog affects neighbors, tell your property manager as well as us.

What affects toilet repair cost

Small repairs are quick. Installs and floor repairs take longer.

  • Part replacedFlappers and fill valves cost less than a full flush mechanism.
  • Floor and flange conditionRot or a broken flange adds repair work.
  • Toilet modelA basic bowl costs less than a tall, comfort-height or wall-hung unit.
  • Shutoff valve conditionOld valves that will not close need replacement.
  • After hoursEmergency timing is priced for that service, and you hear the number first.

Fixture rules and permits

Toilets are installed to the standards of the California Plumbing Code, which San Bruno adopts under section 11.20.010. Permit rules vary with the scope of the work.

The Building Division at 650-616-7058 can tell you whether a replacement at your address needs a permit.
