Before we arrive: what to do in an emergency

These four steps limit damage while an emergency plumber is on the way. They work for most burst pipes, failed supply lines and water heater leaks.

1

Stop the water

Close the main shutoff valve, usually a wheel or lever near where the service line enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

2

Cut power near water

If water is near outlets, appliances or the panel, switch off those breakers only if you can reach them without standing in water.

3

Drain the lines

Open the lowest faucet and flush an upstairs toilet once to relieve pressure. Leave sinks and tubs alone during a sewer backup.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Call (650) 860-1670, describe the problem and send photos of the damage if asked. Move valuables and rugs away from the wet area while you wait.

What makes San Bruno plumbing different

The city buys most of its water from the San Francisco Regional Water System and pumps the rest from its own wells. According to the city's 2023 water quality report, about 29 percent of supply comes from local groundwater. That groundwater averaged 215.6 ppm hardness as calcium carbonate, which is hard enough to build scale inside water heaters over the years.

That is why a tank that is only ten years old can still have a crusted element. It also explains why we check the anode rod and flush sediment on water heater repair calls and talk about a water softener when the numbers support one.

San Bruno also asks owners of homes older than 50 years to have the sewer lateral inspected and certified by the city before the home is sold. A camera sewer inspection shows the condition of that pipe, and sewer line repair follows if the camera finds a crack or offset.

Hidden leaks, high bills and slab problems

A water bill that jumps with no change in habits usually points to a hidden leak. A quick test is to shut off every fixture and watch the meter dial. If it still moves, water is escaping somewhere. Our leak detection technicians trace it with acoustic gear and thermal cameras, so the repair opens the smallest possible patch of floor or wall.

Homes on concrete slabs can leak under the floor, and the signs include a warm spot, the sound of running water or a damp carpet edge. Our slab leak repair page explains the options, from a spot repair to rerouting the line overhead.
