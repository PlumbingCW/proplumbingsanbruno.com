Plumber in Lomita Park, San Bruno, CA

Plumbing help for houses, duplexes and apartments in Lomita Park, along El Camino Real.

Lomita Park is a compact neighborhood along the El Camino Real corridor, just west of San Francisco International Airport. Wikipedia describes it as roughly bounded by San Felipe Avenue, El Camino Real, San Juan Avenue and the railroad tracks. San Bruno annexed it in 1953.

It has a long history. El Camino Real was paved through the area in 1912 as California's first paved state highway, and a post office first opened in 1927 under the name Belmae Park before being renamed Lomita Park in 1933.

A mix of small houses and multi-unit buildings

A listing guide describes mostly one-story homes here, including Spanish-style stucco houses and simple ranch houses, along with duplexes, triplexes and low-rise apartments. It puts the typical house at about 1,286 square feet on a lot near 3,900 square feet.

Sources disagree on how old the housing is, with one listing guide giving a 1930s median and a census-based site pointing to the 1960s. We do not assume an age for any building and look at the pipes themselves.

Rules that apply to older homes

Owners who plan to sell should know about the city's lateral program, which covers homes over 50 years old and has been enforced since May 8, 2015. A sewer inspection can show the pipe's condition first.

Nearby

Another older neighborhood, Mills Park, lies near San Bruno City Park.
