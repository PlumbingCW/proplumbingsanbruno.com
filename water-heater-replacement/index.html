Emergency Water Heater Replacement in San Bruno, CA

A tank that has rusted through cannot be repaired. We answer 24/7, size the replacement for your home and install it with straps, venting and a pressure relief line.

When a tank has rusted through, the question is not repair but how fast you can get hot water back. If your heater is leaking from the base, shut off the cold supply valve and power or gas, then call. We answer 24/7 and plan same-day replacement when stock allows.

Tell us the heater's fuel, size and the room it sits in. A photo of the data plate lets us line up the right unit before we arrive.

Failed water heater: what to do first

  • Shut off the cold water to the heater and turn off power or gas.
  • Drain a few gallons through the drain valve if water is still pooling, using a hose to a safe spot.
  • Move boxes, paint and stored items away from the area.
  • Take a photo of the data plate and the leak.
  • Call us and say whether you want the same fuel type or are considering a change.

Choosing the right replacement

Size matters. A household of two or three often does well with a 40 to 50 gallon tank. Larger families use 50 to 75 gallons, and a unit that is too small runs out of hot water at the worst time.

Fuel matters too. Gas heaters recover quickly, electric units are simple to install and heat pump hybrids use less electricity. We will explain what your home's gas line, panel and ventilation allow.

Local rules: straps, permits and the air district

California law requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake movement. We install strapping on every replacement. Fixed vents, discharge pipes and drain pans are part of the install too.

San Bruno adopts the 2025 California Plumbing Code under section 11.20.010 of its municipal code, and the building official is the administrative authority. Permit rules vary, so ask the Building Division. In the Bay Area, the air district's zero-NOx rule for new residential water heaters was scheduled for January 2027, and the district said draft amendments would move it to January 2028. Check baaqmd.gov for the current date before you buy.

Tank or tankless?

Tankless units save space and heat water on demand, but they cost more up front and need adequate gas supply and venting. A tank is cheaper to install and simple to service. If you are weighing a switch, see tankless water heater for the trade-offs.

For a planned upgrade without an emergency, water heater installation covers relocations, fuel changes and new venting.

Old tanks, hard water and sediment

San Bruno's groundwater averaged 215.6 ppm hardness in the city's 2023 report. In a tank, that hardness settles as scale on the floor and around the heating elements. Over years it cuts efficiency and can cook the tank bottom.

If your old heater was crusty when we drained it, we will talk about a softener or a simple annual flush for the next one.

Total cost is the unit, the labor and any code corrections.

Code points that apply to a replacement

California Health and Safety Code section 19211 requires bracing for new and replacement water heaters, and the California Plumbing Code sets the minimum. San Bruno adopts the 2025 code under section 11.20.010.

A replacement is normally permitted work. We explain who files the permit and how the inspection happens before the day of the install.
