Plumber in Rollingwood, San Bruno, CA

Plumbing help for the mid-century houses of Rollingwood, on San Bruno's foothill side.

Rollingwood is a residential neighborhood on San Bruno's foothill side, with views over downtown. One listing guide gives its general frame as Interstate 280 to the east, Sneath Lane to the south and Skyline Boulevard to the west. Fleetwood Park sits on Skyline Boulevard, and Westborough Square is the shopping center inside the neighborhood.

The city has not published formal borders, so we describe the area by those anchor streets and parks.

Mid-century houses, mostly raised ranch

Rollingwood was one of the neighborhoods that filled with new subdivisions after World War II. A listing guide puts the median year built near 1955 and says most homes date from the 1950s and 1960s, with a few built in the early 2000s. Those are estimates.

The same guide says the typical house is a three-bedroom raised ranch of about 1,300 square feet. That layout often puts a water heater in a garage or a utility closet and the main shutoff near the front of the house. We confirm what is in your house.

What owners tend to call about

Water heater swaps

Mid-century houses often get their second or third heater. We replace like for like or talk through a larger or tankless unit. See water heater replacement and tankless options.

Remodel plumbing

A new bath or kitchen means new valves, traps and sometimes a new drain run. See plumbing installation.

Slow drains

Kitchen and bath drains that slow down again after a snake need a closer look. See drain cleaning and hydro jetting.

Hidden moisture

Stains on a ceiling below a bath or a bill that jumps can point to a leak. See leak detection.

Before you sell

For a Rollingwood house over 50 years old, the city's lateral program means a certificate is needed before the title changes hands. The city page dates enforcement to May 8, 2015.

Planning the inspection early leaves time for any repair. Ask us about a camera look at the lateral, and see sewer line repair for the options if the pipe has a problem.

Nearby

Hillside homes in Portola Highlands list the same nearby parks, Fleetwood Park and Monte Verde Park.
