When a burst pipe is an emergency

Any pipe that sprays, floods a floor or soaks a ceiling is an emergency. So is a supply line that has failed behind a wall, because the damage is hidden until the drywall softens. A slow drip under a sink can usually wait for the next appointment, but a pipe that has split open cannot.

The pipes in a house do not all fail the same way. The table below shows what we usually find in San Bruno homes and what the repair involves.

Pipe materialHow it usually failsTypical repair
CopperPinholes from corrosion, split joints, fittings that lose their solderCut out the bad section and sweat in new copper or use a push fitting
Galvanized steelRust from the inside narrows the pipe until a fitting gives wayReplace the run, since the next section is usually close to failing
PEXCracked fittings, damage from a nail or screw, sun damage where exposedReplace the fitting or section with crimp or expansion connections
CPVCBrittle cracks at fittings after years of heat cyclingReplace the cracked section and nearby fittings
Cast iron drainCracks, rusted-through bottoms, failed hub jointsCut out and replace with ABS or PVC, or couple in new pipe

What we do on a burst pipe call

  1. Stop the waterWe confirm the main shutoff holds and isolate the broken section so the rest of the house can keep water where possible.
  2. Find the breakWe open only the wall or ceiling needed, using a moisture meter and, when the leak is hidden, acoustic and thermal tools.
  3. Repair the pipeThe damaged section is cut back to sound pipe and replaced with the right material and fittings, then pressure tested.
  4. Review what else is at riskIf the pipe around the break is corroded, we show you the condition and explain whether a larger repair makes sense.

Pipe leak repair and cracked pipe repair

Not every failure is a dramatic burst. Many calls start with a damp spot, a stain on a ceiling or a water meter that keeps moving. A pipe leak can sit inside a wall for weeks before it shows. Leak detection finds the exact spot, which keeps the hole in the wall small and the repair cost down.

Copper pipe repair is the most common job we see. Corrosion eats pinholes into thin-walled copper, and a single pinhole usually means the same stretch of pipe has other thin spots. When several leaks appear in the same few feet, we recommend replacing the whole run instead of patching it again.

Drain pipe repair when the break is on the waste side

A cracked drain pipe works differently from a supply break. Water does not spray, it seeps out and soaks the soil, slab or wall cavity. Stains, odors and gurgling are the signs. If the camera shows a cracked or offset section, we repair it, and for the buried section outside the house see our sewer line repair page.

Why pressure matters on San Bruno hills

San Bruno rises from flat ground near the bay into foothills, with Crestmoor above 600 feet and Portola Highlands above 700 feet. The city's water system carries 116 miles of main and 31 pressure regulating stations to hold pressure steady across those elevations, according to the Bay Area Water Supply and Conservation Agency profile.

For a homeowner, that means pressure at your tap can run high on lower streets. High static pressure stresses old fittings and supply valves. When we repair a burst pipe, we check your pressure with a gauge, and we tell you if a pressure regulator is missing or worn.

What affects the cost of burst pipe repair

A burst pipe repair is priced by what has to be done, not by the hour on the clock. These are the factors that move the number.

  • Pipe material and sizeCopper and PEX repairs run differently, and larger lines take more fittings and time.
  • Access to the breakA pipe in an open crawlspace is quick. A pipe behind tile or inside a finished ceiling needs careful opening and patching.
  • Length of damaged pipeOne failed joint is a small job. A corroded run with several pinholes is better replaced as a unit.
  • Water shutoff and valvesIf the main valve or a branch valve will not close, replacing it is part of stopping the leak.
  • After-hours callEmergency work at night or on a weekend can price differently from a planned daytime visit. You hear the number before work starts.

Insurance often covers sudden water damage from a burst pipe, but the repair of the pipe itself is usually the homeowner's cost. Want your real number? Call (650) 860-1670.

If the burst affects the whole line to the house, repiping may be worth pricing next to a series of repairs. For a house in South San Francisco or any nearby town, the process is the same and so is our 24/7 availability.

What to do while you wait for the plumber

  • Close the main shutoff valve and open the lowest faucet to drain the line.
  • Switch off the water heater, and switch off breakers for wet rooms only if you can reach them dry.
  • Move rugs, electronics and valuables out of the wet area.
  • Take photos of the damage for your insurer before you clean up.
  • Do not use a ceiling light or fan under a wet ceiling.

Codes and contacts for pipe repairs

Pipe repairs in San Bruno fall under the California Plumbing Code, which the city adopts in its municipal code. According to the Building Division, permit questions go to 650-616-7058.

If the break is on the street side of the meter, the City Water Division at (650) 616-7162 handles it. A quick look at the meter box usually shows which side the problem is on.
