Emergency Garbage Disposal Repair in San Bruno, CA

A leaking or jammed disposal can flood a kitchen. We answer 24/7, repair what can be saved and replace what cannot.

  • 24/7 emergency line
  • Repair and replace
  • Sink drain checked

A garbage disposal that leaks, hums or backs up the sink needs prompt attention. Turn off the disposal and the wall switch, and do not put your hand inside it. If water is on the floor, close the stop valves under the sink. We answer 24/7 and handle disposals day or night.

Tell us the brand if you know it, and say whether the unit hums, leaks, smells or is jammed. That helps us bring the right parts.

Disposal jammed, humming or leaking: first steps

  1. Switch off the disposal and unplug it, or flip its breaker, before you touch anything.
  2. Use a flashlight and tongs to look for a foreign object. Never use your hand.
  3. Use the hex key at the base to turn the flywheel and free the jam, then press the reset button.
  4. If water is leaking, close the stop valves under the sink and call.

Garbage disposal replacement and installation

A new disposal is a good choice when the old one has failed or leaks from the body. We match horsepower to your use, set the mounting assembly, connect the drain and dishwasher line, and test the unit. A new power cord or a wall switch may be part of the job.

Plan for a half hour to an hour for a swap in the same sink. A first-time install needs electrical work and sometimes a new drain connection. Tell us the layout and we will tell you what to expect.

Kitchen sink clogs that start at the disposal

If the sink backs up when the disposal runs, the blockage is downstream. A cable through the trap arm usually clears it. If the problem returns, grease has built up in the drain line, and a camera shows how far it extends. See drain cleaning for what that involves.

Hard water scale and grease cooperate. The city's 2023 report shows local groundwater hardness at an average of 215.6 ppm, which leaves mineral crust on metal surfaces. Regular cold water flushing after use keeps grease moving.

Kitchens in older San Bruno homes

Older kitchens often have a single drain trap arm running a long way to the wall, with little slope. Food waste and grease settle in the flat section. If your disposal is new but the sink keeps clogging, the drain layout may be the issue.

We can also check the dishwasher air gap or high loop, which keeps dirty water from flowing back into the dishwasher.

Electrical and drain rules

A disposal connects to both the drain and a power supply. Wiring follows the National Electrical Code (NEC), which is why we switch off the breaker and test for power before we touch the unit.

The drain side follows the California Plumbing Code. Never put your hand in a disposal, even when it is off.
