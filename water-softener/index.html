Water Softener and Filtration in San Bruno, CA

San Bruno blends hard local groundwater with softer regional water. We test your tap first and recommend equipment only when the numbers support it.

  • Tap testing
  • Softeners and filters
  • Honest advice

Hard water leaves white crust on faucets, cloudy spots on glasses and scale inside water heaters. Whether it affects your home depends on which water reaches your tap. San Bruno blends surface water from the San Francisco Regional Water System with water from the city's own wells, and the two differ a lot.

This page explains what the city's numbers say, how to test your own water and when a softener or filter is worth installing. It is a planned service, not an emergency one.

What the city's water quality report says

215.6 ppmaverage hardness of local groundwater (range 158 to 324)
46 ppmaverage hardness of San Francisco Regional Water System water
29%share of 2023 supply from city wells

These figures come from the City of San Bruno 2023 Water Quality Report. Hardness is reported as calcium carbonate. The report also lists a groundwater pH of 7.27 and chloramine as the disinfectant, with a running annual average of 2.86 ppm.

The report does not give hardness in grains per gallon. Using the standard conversion of about 17.1 ppm per grain, the groundwater average is roughly 12.6 grains per gallon. That conversion is ours.

The ranges above are a common classification. Your tap may sit anywhere between the two source averages depending on your street and the time of year.

How to know what you actually have

  1. Look for signs: white crust on faucets, spotted glassware, soap that does not lather.
  2. Use a hardness test strip or ask us to test a sample from your kitchen tap.
  3. Ask the city's Water Division which sources serve your area.
  4. Check your water heater. Heavy scale in the drain water is a sign.

Where a softener goes and what installation involves

A softener is installed on the main water line after the shutoff and before the water heater, with a drain connection for regeneration and an outlet for power. Outdoor hose bibs are usually left on hard water.

We plan the location, the bypass valve, the drain and the electrical. Some installs include a pressure check, since a softener adds a small pressure drop.

Who benefits most

Households that rely on a tank water heater benefit the most, since scale shortens tank life and reduces efficiency. A tankless heater needs even more protection, as its heat exchanger fouls quickly in hard water. See tankless water heater for the details.

If your heater is failing from scale, see water heater repair. We will not push equipment where the numbers do not support it.
