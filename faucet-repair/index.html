Emergency Faucet Repair in San Bruno, CA

A faucet that will not shut off or a valve leaking into a wall is urgent. We answer 24/7 and repair faucets, stop valves and shower valves.

  • 24/7 emergency line

A faucet that will not shut off, a supply line that has burst under a sink or a shower valve leaking behind a wall needs quick action. Close the shutoff valve under the sink, or the main valve if the water will not stop, then call. Our 24/7 line is staffed nights and weekends.

Most faucet problems are small, and a quick repair avoids the expensive mess a leak leaves behind.

Faucet leaking or valve stuck: what to do first

  • Turn off the stop valve under the sink. If it will not turn, close the main shutoff.
  • Place a towel and bucket under the leak and clear the cabinet.
  • Do not wrench a seized valve, since it can snap and make the leak worse.
  • Call the 24/7 line and describe where the water is coming from.

Burst or broken supply valves

Stop valves and braided supply lines are the weak links under every sink and behind every toilet. Old ones corrode, crack or fail at the nut. When a stop valve fails, water flows with no way to close it except at the main.

We replace failed valves and lines with quarter-turn stops that are easy to close. If you have multiple old valves, replacing them together is faster than one at a time.

Shower valve, showerhead and tub faucet leaks

A shower that drips after being turned off usually has a worn cartridge. Dripping at the wall behind the handle points to a leaking valve body, and staining on the ceiling below indicates water inside the wall. We open the access panel and test.

Showerheads clog with mineral scale in areas with hard water. Many can be cleaned. When the head or arm is corroded, replacement is better.

Shower and tub drain clogs

If the shower drains slowly, hair and soap film are almost always the cause. A hand auger clears most of them. If water backs up into the tub when other fixtures drain, the clog is deeper and shared. See drain cleaning for those calls.

Hard water and fixtures in San Bruno

Mineral scale shortens the life of cartridges and aerators. The city's 2023 water quality report lists local groundwater at 215.6 ppm hardness, with a range of 158 to 324 ppm. The city also gets water from the San Francisco Regional Water System, which is softer, and the mix at your tap depends on your location.

If your faucets crust over quickly, a water softener may help, but test your water first. We will not recommend one without a reason.

What affects faucet and fixture repair cost

Fixture jobs are quick, but a few factors change the cost.

  • Fixture typeA basic kitchen faucet differs from a pull-down, a wall-mount or a shower valve.
  • Parts availabilityCommon cartridges are easy. Older or specialty valves can take longer to source.
  • AccessA faucet at an open sink is simple. A valve behind a tile wall needs careful opening.
  • Stop valve conditionReplacing seized valves is part of a clean repair.
  • Time of callAfter-hours work is priced for that service. You hear the number first.
