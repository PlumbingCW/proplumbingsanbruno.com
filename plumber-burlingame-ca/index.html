A single-source water system

The profile puts Burlingame's service population at 31,457 across 5.5 square miles, covering the entire city, parts of unincorporated Burlingame Hills and a few properties in San Mateo and Hillsborough. Water from the regional system averaged 46 ppm hardness in San Bruno's 2023 report, which is soft.

Soft water means mineral scale builds slowly. That moves leaks, drain lines and older supply pipe to the front of the list, and it makes tank heaters last longer than they do in harder areas. A water softener is rarely needed.

Burlingame Avenue, Broadway and the neighborhoods

Burlingame Avenue and Broadway are the main retail and downtown districts, and Burlingame Station, a Caltrain stop on the National Register of Historic Places, anchors the area. Neighborhoods include Burlingame Hills, Easton Addition and Ray Park. The city covers 6.04 square miles, and 27.25 percent of that area is water, since it fronts San Francisco Bay.

Restaurants along those streets build grease in their lines, and older buildings share drains. For after-hours help, see drain cleaning and hydro jetting.

Housing and renters

Burlingame had 13,170 housing units in 2020 and 46.1 percent were owner-occupied, so more than half of occupied homes are rented. That mix means many calls come from tenants and property managers as well as owners.

We explain the cause, the options and the price before work begins, and we keep notes that landlords, tenants and insurers can use.

Restaurants, shops and offices

Burlingame Avenue is a dining and shopping street, and the commercial calls that come from it look different from home calls. A restaurant needs grease handled before it reaches the sewer, floor drains that carry water away quickly and a water heater that can keep up at the dinner rush.

Retail and office buildings call about restrooms, backflow assemblies and slow main lines. We schedule planned work outside business hours when we can, and we answer emergencies at any hour. See commercial plumbing and hydro jetting for the services behind those calls.
