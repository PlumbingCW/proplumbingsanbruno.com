Emergency Drain Cleaning in San Bruno, CA

Slow drains, gurgling pipes and backups clear faster when you act early. We answer 24/7 and send a plumber with the right machine for your line.

  • 24/7 emergency line
  • Snaking and jetting
  • Camera when needed

A clogged drain gets worse every time you run water. If a sink, tub or toilet is backing up right now, stop using water in the house, keep the drain stopper clear and do not pour chemical cleaner into a standing clog. Our emergency drain cleaning crews work 24/7, nights and weekends included.

Tell us on the phone which fixtures are slow and which are fully blocked. That lets us bring the right cable machine on the first trip and, if the main line is involved, bring a camera too.

Clogged drain: what to do first

  • Stop running water in the sink, tub and washer that share the clogged line.
  • Use a plunger on a single fixture with the overflow opening blocked by a wet rag.
  • Do not mix drain cleaners. Fumes from mixed chemicals are dangerous in a closed bathroom.
  • If a floor drain or the lowest tub is bubbling, treat it as a main line clog and call right away.

Kitchen sink and disposal clogs

Kitchen drains clog from grease, starch and coffee grounds. Rice, pasta and potato peels swell inside the trap and arm, and a double sink with a disposal gives them even more places to catch. We clear the line with a cable that matches the pipe size, then run water to prove the pipe flows.

If the sink drains slowly after a cleaning, the cause is often downstream. A long horizontal run with too little slope holds water, and grease settles in the low spot. We can inspect it with a camera and recommend a fix. A jam in the disposal itself is a separate repair, covered under garbage disposal repair.

Shower and tub drain clogs

Hair is the main cause. It wraps around the strainer cross and the first few inches of the drain, then binds with soap film. Many of these clear with a small hand auger. If the tub drains fine until the toilet runs, the problem is deeper and shares a line.

A tub that gurgles when the washing machine drains points to a partial blockage in a shared branch. We trace it by checking which fixtures react, then clear it from the nearest cleanout or access point.

Snaking, cabling and when to choose jetting

Cable (snaking)

  • Best for single clogs, hair, soft grease and paper
  • Pierces the blockage and opens a channel
  • Lower cost and quick
  • Leaves some pipe wall coating behind

Hydro jetting

  • Best for heavy grease, scale and roots in main lines
  • Scrubs the full pipe wall with high pressure water
  • Takes longer and needs access
  • See hydro jetting for when it is worth it

Drain line leaks and slow drains that clear then return

A drain that clears and then returns within weeks is not a clog problem anymore. It means the pipe has a defect such as a sag, a root intrusion at a joint or a cracked section that lets water out and debris in. A short camera run finds it.

Drain line leaks show up as damp wall bases, stains below bathrooms or a smell that comes and goes. Fixing the line, not just clearing it, is the only way to stop the cycle. For the buried part of the system, see sewer line cleaning.

A note on older San Bruno homes

San Bruno was incorporated in 1914 and grew in waves after that, so a single street can mix cast iron, clay and plastic drain lines. Older cast iron scales inside and catches grease and paper. Clay laterals can take root at joints.

The city asks owners of homes older than 50 years to have the sewer lateral inspected and certified by the city before a sale. If a camera run for a stubborn drain turns up damage, the same footage is useful when that certificate comes due.

What affects drain cleaning prices

Most drain jobs are quick, but a few things push the price up or down.

  • Where the clog sitsA trap or fixture drain is a short job. A main line clog reached through a cleanout takes more cable and time.
  • Access to the lineA missing or buried cleanout adds work, and sometimes a toilet has to be pulled to reach the line.
  • Type of blockageHair and paper clear easily. Roots and hard grease need heavier tools.
  • Camera inspectionLooking inside the pipe after cleaning costs extra but tells you whether the clog will return.
  • Time of dayEmergency calls at night or on a weekend are priced for that service, and you hear the price first.

Who owns which part of the sewer

According to the City of San Bruno, sewer service and the sewer lateral program sit with Public Works Wastewater. If a clog is in the city main rather than in your lateral, the city is the right contact.

A camera run tells us which side the blockage is on, so you do not pay to clear a line that belongs to someone else.
