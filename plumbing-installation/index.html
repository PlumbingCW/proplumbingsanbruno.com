Plumbing Installation in San Bruno, CA

New fixtures, new lines and remodels need drain slope, venting and permits done right. We plan the layout and install to the California Plumbing Code.

  • Permits handled
  • Fixtures and lines
  • Code-compliant

Plumbing installation covers anything new: a bathroom addition, a kitchen remodel, a laundry hookup, a new water line or an appliance connection. Done right, it is quiet, tight and built to code. Done wrong, it leaks inside a wall for years.

This is planned work, so we schedule it during regular hours. For emergencies, our 24/7 line is open and we answer it day or night.

What a plumbing installation job includes

  1. Plan and permitWe review the layout, drain slopes, vent paths, water supply and code requirements, then handle permit steps.
  2. Rough-inDrain, vent and supply lines go in before walls close, and they are pressure and leak tested.
  3. InspectionThe Building Division inspects the rough-in where required.
  4. Fixtures and finishFixtures, valves and trim are set and tested, and we show you the shutoffs.

Gas line safety comes first

A gas smell is an emergency for the utility, not a plumbing call. If you smell gas, leave the building, avoid anything that can spark, call 911 and then PG&E at 1-800-743-5000. PG&E also lists 1-877-660-6789 as its 24-hour customer service line.

For planned gas piping, such as a new line to a water heater, range or dryer, we size the pipe to the load, install shutoffs and test the system. Gas work needs a permit and inspection, so ask the Building Division what applies.

Water service lines and pressure

The water service line runs from the meter to your house. If it leaks or restricts flow, you may see low pressure or high bills. We can replace the private-side line, and the city handles its side. Call 811 before digging, as PG&E reminds customers.

San Bruno's system runs 116 miles of water main and 31 pressure regulating stations across hills and flats, so static pressure varies by street. A house with high pressure should have a working regulator. A house with low pressure may have a failing service line, a closed valve or clogged galvanized pipe.

Remodels, additions and new construction

A bathroom or kitchen remodel often moves drains and supplies. Drain slope and vent routing are the details that decide whether a fixture drains well. We plan them before the walls close.

For new construction, we work from plans, install rough-ins in stages and coordinate with inspections. The Building Division at 567 El Camino Real enforces the California Plumbing Code adopted by the city, and it runs the SB Permits online system.

Code and permit basics

New fixtures and new lines are installed to the California Plumbing Code, and according to that code adding a fixture often means venting and drain sizing checks, which is why a permit and an inspection are part of the job.

The Building Division at 650-616-7058 explains what applies, and its SB Permits portal handles the application.
