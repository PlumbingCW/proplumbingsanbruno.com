Fog, rain and damp crawlspaces

Summer fog brings light drizzle overnight and in the morning, and winter rain soaks the hillsides. Houses with crawlspaces can hold moisture for months, hiding a leaking supply line or a failed drain until the floor feels soft. A damp crawlspace also masks the smell of a slow leak.

We inspect with a light and a moisture meter, and we trace hidden leaks with leak detection tools before any floor is opened.

Linda Mar, Sharp Park, Rockaway Beach and Pedro Point

Pacifica's neighborhoods include Linda Mar, Sharp Park, Rockaway Beach, Pedro Point, Fairmont and Vallemar, and the city straddles San Pedro Creek, which flows from the western slope of Sweeney Ridge. Pacifica State Beach, Mori Point and the Pacifica Pier draw visitors and shape the commercial strip.

Homes near the creek and the beach live with higher moisture than homes on drier ridges. Of occupied homes, 67.8 percent are owner-occupied, so most calls come from owners protecting a long-term investment.

Water from the Peninsula's reservoirs

The North Coast County Water District report says all drinking water it delivered in 2025 was purchased from the San Francisco Public Utilities Commission. Nearly all of it came from Crystal Springs and San Andreas Reservoirs and was treated at the Harry Tracy Water Treatment Plant in San Bruno.

The report lists hardness from 8.1 to 112 ppm with an average of 62 ppm, which is soft. Scale is rarely the issue in Pacifica, so a water softener is seldom needed. Leaks, drains and old pipes come first.

Rain, drainage and exterior plumbing

Pacifica gets about 30 inches of rain a year, more than the drier inland side of the Peninsula, and that water has to go somewhere. Gutters, yard drains and foundation drainage matter as much as the pipes inside. A downspout that dumps beside a foundation can put water where the house does not want it.

Exterior fittings, hose bibs and outdoor shutoff valves live in damp air and tend to wear faster than indoor parts. A quick look each fall for drips and corrosion is worth the few minutes. If you find a leak outside, shut off the supply to that line and call us.

Homes on slopes can also see water pressure that varies with elevation. A pressure gauge on a hose bib tells you what your fixtures are living with, and plumbing installation covers adding a regulator where pressure runs high.
