About San Bruno Pro Plumbing

A 24/7 emergency plumbing line based on El Camino Real, built around diagnosing first and pricing before work starts.

About us

A local emergency plumbing line for the northern Peninsula

San Bruno Pro Plumbing runs a 24/7 emergency line for San Bruno and the towns around it. The office is at 1202 El Camino Real, in the middle of the corridor that connects San Bruno to South San Francisco and Millbrae.

The idea is simple. When a pipe bursts or a sewer backs up, you should reach a person who understands the problem, tells you what to do in the first minutes and sends a licensed plumber who explains the price before the work starts.

How we work

Diagnose first

A technician finds the cause before anyone talks about a price, so you are not paying to guess.

Clear price, then work

You hear the cost and the plan before anything is cut or replaced. If an open wall changes that, we stop and explain.

Explain in plain terms

We say what failed, why it failed and what the choices are. You decide.

Leave it clean

Work areas are covered and cleaned. Old parts are available to see on request.

Why local knowledge changes the advice

San Bruno's water comes from two places. According to the city's 2023 water quality report, supply is regional system water and groundwater from city wells, which supply about 29 percent. The groundwater averages 215.6 ppm of hardness, while the regional water averages 46 ppm. That difference explains why some heaters scale up and others do not.

The city also requires an inspection and certification of the sewer lateral before a home over 50 years old is sold. We know how that rule affects sellers and buyers, so we can record a camera inspection that is useful beyond the repair itself.

The hills matter too. Homes in Crestmoor and Portola Highlands sit well above the flat streets near the bay, so water pressure and sewer slope are different from a house near El Camino Real.

What happens on a call

  1. A person answersThe dispatcher asks for your address and what is happening.
  2. First steps start at onceWe coach you through shutting off water or appliances while the plumber is arranged.
  3. The cause is foundThe technician checks the problem before talking about a price.
  4. You decideYou hear the plan and the price, and work starts only when you agree.

What we do and do not claim

We send licensed and insured plumbers. We do not publish made-up awards, star ratings or customer quotes, because they would be hard for you to check. If you want to check a contractor, the California Contractors State License Board has a lookup by license number or business name, and it shows complaint disclosures.

We also do not promise arrival times. Traffic, the time of day and the number of calls all change the drive. What we do promise is that the phone is answered, the first steps start right away and a plumber is sent the same day.

How to compare any plumber, including us

  • Look the license up on the California Contractors State License Board site.
  • Ask for the scope of work in writing before it starts.
  • Ask who pulls the permit and who meets the inspector.
  • Ask what the warranty covers, and for how long.
  • Ask whether old parts will be shown to you.
  • Be careful with any phone price given before the cause is known.

A plumber who answers these plainly is usually one who has done the job before. We expect to be asked.

City offices that matter to plumbing work

Many plumbing jobs touch a city office at some point. The San Bruno Water Division handles water service questions at (650) 616-7162. Utility Billing, at (650) 616-7086, handles water bills, and sewer charges moved to the San Mateo County property tax bill on July 1, 2024. The Building Division, at 650-616-7058, handles permits.

We tell customers which office to contact when a question is not ours to answer.
