What to have ready

  • The address of the problem and the nearest cross street.
  • What failed, and whether water or sewage is still flowing.
  • Where your main shutoff valve is, if you know.
  • Photos of the damage, if you can take them safely.
  • Whether the home is rented, so we can coordinate access.

Emergency steps while you wait

  1. Stop the waterClose the main shutoff valve, usually near where the service line enters the house.
  2. Protect the electricsIf water is near outlets or the panel, switch off those breakers only if you can reach them dry.
  3. Drain pressureOpen the lowest faucet to relieve the line, and leave sinks alone during a sewer backup.
  4. Gas smellAccording to PG&E, you should leave the building, call 911 and then call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000. Call us once the utility says it is safe.
