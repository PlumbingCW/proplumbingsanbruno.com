Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit this site or call our 24/7 line.

What this policy covers

This page explains how San Bruno Pro Plumbing handles information when you visit this website or call our line. We keep it short because the site is simple. There are no accounts, no forms and no online payments.

Information you give us by phone or email

When you call, we may ask for your name, address, phone number and a description of the problem so we can send a plumber. Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you email us, we receive the address you write from and whatever you include in the message.

We use this information to answer your request, schedule and perform work, and follow up on it. We do not sell it.

Information the website collects

The site does not ask you to create an account or fill in a form. It loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on some pages, an embedded Google map. Those services receive your IP address and browser details when the content loads, and their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.

Sharing

We share information only with the people who need it to do the job, such as the technician assigned to your call, and where the law requires it. We do not share it for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing contact@proplumbingsanbruno.com. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version appears here with a new date. This version was last updated on November 12, 2026.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to contact@proplumbingsanbruno.com or to San Bruno Pro Plumbing, 1202 El Camino Real, San Bruno, CA 94066.
