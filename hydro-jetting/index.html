Emergency Hydro Jetting in San Bruno, CA

When a cable will not clear a main line, high pressure water usually will. We jet 24/7 and check the pipe by camera when it matters.

  • 24/7 emergency line
  • Grease and root removal
  • Camera verified

If a cable has not cleared your main line, or sewage is coming up in the lowest drain in the house, that is a hydro jetting situation and it cannot wait. We run emergency jetting 24/7. Stop using water, keep people and pets away from the backed-up area and call the line.

Tell us if the water is clear, gray or black. That helps us decide whether the problem is a simple blockage or a collapsed section that needs repair before jetting.

Sewer backup: what to do first

  1. Stop all water use, including the washer, dishwasher and showers.
  2. Do not flush toilets, since the water has nowhere to go.
  3. Keep children and pets away from the affected area. Wear boots and gloves if you must walk through it.
  4. Open windows for ventilation and call us. Photos of the backup help your insurer later.

How hydro jetting works

A jetting machine pumps water through a hose with a rotating nozzle. The nozzle's rear-facing jets pull the hose forward while a forward jet breaks the blockage. As we pull the hose back, the nozzle scours grease, scale and roots off the pipe wall.

We select the nozzle for the job. A root-cutting head differs from a grease-scouring head, and both differ from a light-pressure head for fragile pipe.

Grease, roots and scale: what jetting clears

Grease builds in layers inside a kitchen branch and in main lines serving restaurants and apartment buildings. Roots grow into joints where a tiny leak gives them water, then thicken until the pipe is a net of fibers. Scale is common in older cast iron and rough clay.

Jetting clears all three, but only a camera can tell you whether the pipe is healthy afterward. We run one after the jetting when it matters, which is covered on the sewer camera inspection page.

Jetting for homes and small businesses in San Bruno

Older homes along the El Camino Real corridor can have long clay or cast iron laterals, and roots are a common reason those lines slow down. Small restaurants and cafes build grease faster than a house does.

We jet residential laterals, shared building lines and commercial kitchen drains. For a restaurant or office with recurring problems, see our commercial plumbing page for scheduled maintenance options.

What affects hydro jetting prices

Jetting costs more than snaking because it needs a heavier machine and more time. These factors set the price.

  • Line length and diameterA short 4 inch lateral is a smaller job than a long run with several branches.
  • AccessA good cleanout makes jetting simple. Without one, we may need to pull a toilet or open an access point.
  • Condition of the pipeA camera inspection before jetting is wise on older pipe and adds to the visit.
  • Severity of the blockageHeavy roots or hard grease can take several passes.
  • Emergency timingAfter-hours jetting is priced for that response. The number is given before work begins.

Pressure guidelines and the lateral rule

Jetting follows the equipment maker's guidelines for pressure and nozzle choice. Cast iron, clay and plastic tolerate different pressures, which is why a camera run comes first.

The city's lateral rule for homes over 50 years old is described on its Public Works Wastewater page. A jetted and recorded line gives you a clear view of the pipe before a sale.
