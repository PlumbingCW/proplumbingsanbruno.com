Emergency Commercial Plumbing in San Bruno, CA

Water on the floor shuts a business down. We answer commercial plumbing calls 24/7 and work around your hours and your landlord.

  • 24/7 emergency line
  • Drains, leaks, heaters
  • Grease and backflow

When a restaurant's kitchen floods or a retail restroom backs up, every hour costs money. We handle commercial plumbing emergencies 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Call, tell us the type of business and the problem, and we send a licensed plumber the same day.

If you can, shut off the main valve or the affected branch, protect electrical equipment and keep customers away from wet floors.

Commercial plumbing emergency: first steps

  • Shut off water at the main or branch valve and note where it is for the plumber.
  • Stop using the affected restrooms and sinks, and post signs.
  • Protect equipment and inventory from water.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell us whether the problem is supply, drain or the water heater.
  • Photograph damage for your insurer and landlord.

Commercial drains and grease

Restaurants build grease faster than a home does. A grease trap or interceptor catches fats before they reach the sewer, but it needs scheduled cleaning. Neglected traps overflow, smell and back up.

We clean traps and the lines that feed them, and for heavy buildup we use hydro jetting. For ordinary clogs in restrooms and prep sinks, drain cleaning is usually enough.

Commercial leak detection, honestly

A high water bill is a common reason for a commercial leak call. The cause is often a running flushometer, a leaking toilet or an irrigation line. We test each before suggesting anything bigger. Some buildings have a leak that cannot be found without opening ceilings, and some turn out to be normal use.

We will tell you what we find and what we did not find. If a visit does not locate a leak, we say so and explain what to try next.

Backflow testing and the water division

Backflow assemblies protect the public water supply. The City of San Bruno Water Division handles water service questions at (650) 616-7162, and it can tell you whether your service has a testing requirement. We test and repair assemblies when a schedule or a failed test requires it.

Businesses around El Camino Real and Tanforan

San Bruno's commercial areas include shops and restaurants along El Camino Real and the retail center on the former Tanforan racetrack site, with BART and Caltrain stations next to it. Older strip buildings share drain lines and water heaters between units.

When one tenant's drain backs up, the cause may sit in a shared line. We coordinate with landlords and neighbors so the repair covers the real cause.

Backflow, permits and city contacts

Many commercial properties must keep backflow assemblies tested on a schedule, according to the water provider's rules. The City of San Bruno Water Division at (650) 616-7162 can tell you whether your service has a testing requirement.

Tenant improvements and new fixtures usually need a permit. The Building Division is at 567 El Camino Real, and its phone is 650-616-7058.
