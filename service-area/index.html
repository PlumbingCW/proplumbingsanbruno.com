San Bruno neighborhoods

The city rises from flat ground near the bay into foothills, and the plumbing changes with the elevation. We work in Crestmoor, Mills Park, Rollingwood, Portola Highlands and Lomita Park, along with the blocks around El Camino Real, Tanforan and Skyline College.

Crestmoor sits above 600 feet and Portola Highlands above 700 feet, so homes there can see higher water pressure than homes down the hill. A pressure check and, where needed, a regulator or expansion tank protect fixtures and the water heater. Lomita Park was annexed in 1953 and has some of the city's older housing.

These differences are why a recommendation in one town does not always fit the next. A water softener makes sense where hardness is high, and it does little where the water is already soft. We say so when the numbers do not support the purchase.

Who to call about water and sewer service

Some problems belong to the utility, not to a plumber. If water is leaking on the street side of the meter, or you have a pressure or quality question, call the utility first. In San Bruno, the Water Division is at (650) 616-7162 and Utility Billing is at (650) 616-7086.

In South San Francisco, the Cal Water Bayshore District is at (650) 558-7800. In Pacifica, the North Coast County Water District is at (650) 355-3462. Millbrae, Daly City and Burlingame each run their own city or district offices, listed on their websites.

A rule of thumb helps. The utility owns the main and the meter. The homeowner owns the pipe from the meter to the house and everything inside it. When you are not sure where the line ends, we can look at it with you and tell you which side the problem is on.

Building permits across the area

Water heater swaps, repipes and sewer repairs often need a permit, and each city has its own building office. In San Bruno, the Building Division is at 567 El Camino Real, 650-616-7058, with a counter open Monday to Thursday from 7:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Other towns post their own hours and portals.

Tell us the town when you call. We plan the permit step for the right office, so the work is inspected where it should be.
